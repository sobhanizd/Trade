# CryptoPilot AI — live demo

Your own private copy of CryptoPilot is starting in this codespace: a PostgreSQL database,
the web app and the background worker that fetches **real market data**.

1. Wait until the terminal shows **"CryptoPilot AI is running"** (about 5 minutes the first
   time, a few seconds after that).
2. A browser tab opens on the login page. If it doesn't: open the **Ports** tab, find port
   **3000** and click the globe icon.
3. Sign in with username **`demo`** and password **`cryptopilot-demo`** (already filled in).

The first market scan takes about a minute; refresh the dashboard if a coin still shows
"no data yet". Everything you do here stays in this codespace.

Stop the codespace when you are done (GitHub → Codespaces → ⋯ → Stop) so it doesn't use your
free hours. It never trades and nothing here is financial advice.
